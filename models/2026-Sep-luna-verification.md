# Complete verification of the latest Luna run

Historical 25-record pilot audit. Superseded by the complete 1,185-CVE run documented in 2026-Sep-full-inference.md; retained only as review history.

Reviewed 2026-09-10 by Codex. Scope: all 25 entries in inference/2026-Sep-luna.jsonl, including every factor, CVSS basis, product scope, available description/FAQ, threat snapshot, and mitigation candidate. This is a review of stored evidence (MSRC revision and EPSS date retained in each JSONL record), not a new fetch or a claim that 1,185 CVEs received model inference. The other 1,160 records retain fallback assessments.

## Findings and changes

- Retained all 25 baseline action recommendations after source review: 3 Immediate, 18 Out-of-cycle, 4 Defer and review.
- Removed perimeter-removal credit for the three explicitly in-network exploit paths (69769, 69829, 69845). Segmentation remains conditional on the actual vulnerable route.
- Removed Protected View credit from two RTF parser cases. The evidence did not establish that the selected policy prevents the parser flaw.
- Removed quantified credit for generic EDR/ASR on both active-exploitation cases. Their advisory text does not bound post-compromise impact or establish a matching prevention rule.
- Set consequence credit to zero for delivery/reachability controls: preventing delivery or restricting sources does not independently reduce host impact after successful exploitation.
- Clarified conditional filtering coverage and remaining bypass routes. Corrected content-trigger explanations, Mac update availability, Outlook FAQ uncertainty, and the Shell/Print workload descriptions; removed their unsupported shared-service inferred tags.
- Aligned the four already-mitigated hosted-service outputs with the displayed operational Low evidence band. Source threat/CVSS metrics remain unchanged; no-customer-action is the reason no patch work is generated.
- Runtime public-evidence floors now survive stale saved inference. Ordinary mitigations retain at least a Scheduled patch obligation. Imported incompatible effects are rejected at runtime as well as in the merge validator.

The framework remains qualitative and model-led. These checks enforce source fidelity and limits on mitigation claims, not an additive risk score. A remaining preventive credit assumes that the operator has verified the stated route or content-blocking conditions; merely selecting a generic platform does not establish that evidence.

## Validation

The repeatable audit checks overlay-to-publication equality, exact CVSS basis, and review provenance for all 25 entries. It exercises all 85 combinations of their candidate controls and all 20,145 individual catalogue selections across 1,185 published records. Evidence-specific regressions cover the removed credits, Microsoft/EPSS increases after inference, and incompatible imported control effects. These are executable behavior checks; semantic findings above come from reviewing the advisory text.

No source CVSS, product list, Microsoft exploitation status, KEV or EPSS fields were intentionally changed. Codex verification provenance is stored separately from the original Luna model and generation timestamp. The source snapshot has unresolved Outlook pane guidance; the assessment now records low confidence rather than claiming that ambiguity has been resolved.
